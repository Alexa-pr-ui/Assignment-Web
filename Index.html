Our Mission

San Joaquin Valley Town is a no-profit organization that is run by an all volunteer board of directors. Our mission is to bring nationally and internationally renowned, thought-provoking speakers who inform, educate and entertain our audience! As one of our members told us: "Each year I give a ticket package to each of our family members. I think of it as the gift of knowledge..and that is priceless"
